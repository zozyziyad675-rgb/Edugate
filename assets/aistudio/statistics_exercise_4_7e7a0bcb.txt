20 MCQs from Lecture 4: Confidence Intervals

---

Q1. A numerical summary of a sample is called a:
A) Parameter
B) Statistic
C) Population
D) Confidence level
Answer: B) Statistic

---

Q2. A numerical summary of a population is called a:
A) Statistic
B) Parameter
C) Point estimate
D) Sample mean
Answer: B) Parameter

---

Q3. A statistic used to estimate a parameter is called a:
A) Confidence interval
B) Margin of error
C) Point estimate
D) Significance level
Answer: C) Point estimate

---

Q4. The sample mean \bar{X} and sample proportion are examples of:
A) Parameters
B) Point estimates
C) Confidence levels
D) Population variances
Answer: B) Point estimates

---

Q5. The purpose of a confidence interval is to:
A) Replace the point estimate
B) Provide a margin of error for the point estimate
C) Eliminate sampling error
D) Calculate the population size
Answer: B) Provide a margin of error for the point estimate

---

Q6. A two-sided confidence interval for the population mean generally has the form:
A) Point Estimate × Margin of Error
B) Point Estimate ± Margin of Error
C) Point Estimate ÷ Margin of Error
D) Margin of Error ± Point Estimate
Answer: B) Point Estimate ± Margin of Error

---

Q7. A confidence level of 100(1-\alpha)\% corresponds to a significance level of:
A) 1-\alpha
B) \alpha
C) \alpha/2
D) 2\alpha
Answer: B) \alpha

---

Q8. Which of the following is NOT a common confidence level?
A) 90%
B) 95%
C) 99%
D) 75%
Answer: D) 75%

---

Q9. For a large sample size (n \geq 30), the confidence interval for \mu uses:
A) The t-distribution
B) The z-distribution (standard normal)
C) The chi-square distribution
D) The F-distribution
Answer: B) The z-distribution (standard normal)

---

Q10. The standard error of the sample mean is given by:
A) \sigma \cdot \sqrt{n}
B) \sigma / \sqrt{n}
C) \sigma^2 / n
D) s^2 / \sqrt{n}
Answer: B) \sigma / \sqrt{n}

---

Q11. \bar{X} \pm 1.645 \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}} is a:
A) 68% confidence interval
B) 90% confidence interval
C) 95% confidence interval
D) 99% confidence interval
Answer: B) 90% confidence interval

---

Q12. \bar{X} \pm 1.96 \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}} is a:
A) 68% confidence interval
B) 90% confidence interval
C) 95% confidence interval
D) 99% confidence interval
Answer: C) 95% confidence interval

---

Q13. \bar{X} \pm 2.58 \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}} is a:
A) 68% confidence interval
B) 90% confidence interval
C) 95% confidence interval
D) 99% confidence interval
Answer: D) 99% confidence interval

---

Q14. For the battery example (\bar{X} = 185.5, s = 5.0, n = 100), the standard error is:
A) 5.0
B) 0.5
C) 0.05
D) 1.0
Answer: B) 0.5 (s/\sqrt{n} = 5/10)

---

Q15. For an 85% confidence interval, \alpha/2 equals:
A) 0.15
B) 0.075
C) 0.025
D) 0.05
Answer: B) 0.075

---

Q16. The critical value z_{0.075} for an 85% confidence interval is:
A) 1.96
B) 1.645
C) 1.44
D) 2.58
Answer: C) 1.44

---

Q17. For the battery example, the 85% confidence interval is:
A) (184.78, 186.22)
B) (184.50, 186.50)
C) (185.00, 186.00)
D) (184.28, 186.72)
Answer: A) (184.78, 186.22)

---

Q18. In Example 2.2 (\bar{x} = 5, \sigma^2 = 9, n = 100), the 95% confidence interval is:
A) (4.412, 5.588)
B) (4.500, 5.500)
C) (4.706, 5.294)
D) (3.412, 6.588)
Answer: A) (4.412, 5.588)

---

Q19. The margin of error of a confidence interval is:
A) \bar{X} + z_{\alpha/2}\sigma/\sqrt{n}
B) z_{\alpha/2}\sigma/\sqrt{n}
C) \sigma/\sqrt{n}
D) z_{\alpha/2}\sigma
Answer: B) z_{\alpha/2}\sigma/\sqrt{n}

---

Q20. To obtain a 99% confidence interval of width ±1.0 A (with s = 5.0), the required sample size is approximately:
A) 100
B) 167
C) 25
D) 250
Answer: B) 167 (using n = z^2\sigma^2/w^2)
