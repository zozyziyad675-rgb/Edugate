Based on the provided lecture slides, here are 20 multiple-choice questions covering numbering systems, conversions, and arithmetic operations.

1. What is the base of the Hexadecimal numbering system?
A) 2
B) 8
C) 10
D) 16
Correct Answer: D) 16

2. In the decimal system, what does the weight of a digit represent?
A) The base multiplied by the position
B) 10 raised to the power of the digit's position
C) The sum of all digits to its right
D) The digit multiplied by 2
Correct Answer: B) 10 raised to the power of the digit's position

3. Which of the following symbols is NOT valid in the octal number system?
A) 7
B) 5
C) 8
D) 0
Correct Answer: C) 8

4. Convert the octal number (721.34)₈ to decimal. What is the weight of the digit '3'?
A) 8²
B) 8¹
C) 8⁻¹
D) 8⁻²
Correct Answer: C) 8⁻¹

5. When performing addition in the octal system, if the sum of a column is equal to or greater than 8, what is the rule?
A) Subtract 8 and carry 1 to the next position
B) Subtract 10 and carry 1 to the next position
C) Add 8 and carry 1 to the next position
D) Reset to 0 and carry 2 to the next position
Correct Answer: A) Subtract 8 and carry 1 to the next position

6. In the Hexadecimal system, what decimal value does the symbol 'B' represent?
A) 10
B) 11
C) 12
D) 15
Correct Answer: B) 11

7. What is the correct expansion for the hexadecimal number (B4A5.3F1)₁₆?
A) B×16³ + 4×16² + A×16¹ + 5×16⁰ + 3×16⁻¹ + F×16⁻² + 1×16⁻³
B) B×16⁴ + 4×16³ + A×16² + 5×16¹ + 3×16⁰ + F×16⁻¹ + 1×16⁻²
C) B×10³ + 4×10² + A×10¹ + 5×10⁰ + 3×10⁻¹ + F×10⁻² + 1×10⁻³
D) B×16³ + 4×16² + A×16¹ + 5×16⁰ + 3×16¹ + F×16² + 1×16³
Correct Answer: A) B×16³ + 4×16² + A×16¹ + 5×16⁰ + 3×16⁻¹ + F×16⁻² + 1×16⁻³

8. What are the only valid symbols in the Binary system?
A) 0, 1, 2
B) 0, 1
C) 1, 2
D) 0 through 7
Correct Answer: B) 0, 1

9. Convert the binary number (1011.101)₂ to decimal. What is the value of the leftmost '1' (at position 2³)?
A) 1
B) 4
C) 8
D) 16
Correct Answer: C) 8

10. In binary addition, what is the result of 1 + 1 + 1?
A) 0 with a carry of 1
B) 1 with a carry of 1
C) 1 with a carry of 0
D) 0 with a carry of 0
Correct Answer: B) 1 with a carry of 1

11. When converting a decimal fraction (e.g., 0.6875) to binary, which operation is repeatedly performed?
A) Division by 2
B) Multiplication by 2
C) Subtraction of 2
D) Addition of 2
Correct Answer: B) Multiplication by 2

12. When converting the integer part of a decimal number to binary, in what order should the remainders be read to form the final binary number?
A) From top to bottom
B) From bottom to top
C) In random order
D) Only the first remainder is used
Correct Answer: B) From bottom to top

13. To convert an octal number to binary, how many binary digits (bits) are equivalent to one octal digit?
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 8
Correct Answer: B) 3

14. Convert the octal number (721.534)₈ to binary. What is the binary equivalent of the digit '7'?
A) 100
B) 101
C) 110
D) 111
Correct Answer: D) 111

15. To convert a hexadecimal number to binary, how many binary digits are equivalent to one hexadecimal digit?
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 16
Correct Answer: C) 4

16. What is the binary equivalent of the hexadecimal digit 'F'?
A) 1111
B) 1010
C) 1100
D) 1000
Correct Answer: A) 1111

17. According to the conversion flowchart, what is the direct method to convert from Hexadecimal to Octal (without going through decimal)?
A) Multiply by 16 then divide by 8
B) Convert Hex to Binary, then Binary to Octal
C) Convert Hex to Decimal, then Decimal to Octal
D) There is no direct method
Correct Answer: B) Convert Hex to Binary, then Binary to Octal

18. What is the weight of the 4th position to the left of the decimal point in the binary system (position 3)?
A) 2
B) 4
C) 8
D) 16
Correct Answer: C) 8

19. In the equation (N)₁₀ = 18² + 28¹ + 78⁰ + 38⁻¹ + 18⁻² + 48⁻³, what is the value of N in octal notation?
A) (127.314)₈
B) (127.413)₈
C) (721.314)₈
D) (127.134)₈
Correct Answer: A) (127.314)₈

20. When converting the decimal number (125.852)₁₀ to binary, the integer part results in (1111101)₂. What is the first step in calculating the fractional part?
A) 0.852 / 2
B) 0.852 + 2
C) 0.852 * 2
D) 0.852 - 2
Correct Answer: C) 0.852 * 2
