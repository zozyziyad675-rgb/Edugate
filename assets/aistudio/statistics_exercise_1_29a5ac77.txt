20 MCQs from Lecture 1: Frequency Distributions

---

Q1. Statistics is defined as the science of collecting, organizing, presenting, analyzing, and interpreting data to assist in making more effective:
A) Observations
B) Decisions
C) Measurements
D) Samples
Answer: B) Decisions

---

Q2. Which branch of statistics uses methods to estimate a property of a population on the basis of a sample?
A) Descriptive statistics
B) Inferential statistics
C) Qualitative statistics
D) Frequency statistics
Answer: B) Inferential statistics

---

Q3. The entire set of individuals or objects of interest is called:
A) A sample
B) A population
C) A variable
D) A frequency
Answer: B) A population

---

Q4. A portion or part of the population of interest is called:
A) A sample
B) A population
C) A class
D) A variable
Answer: A) A sample

---

Q5. A variable observed and recorded as a non-numeric characteristic or attribute is:
A) Quantitative
B) Discrete
C) Qualitative
D) Continuous
Answer: C) Qualitative

---

Q6. Which of the following is an example of a qualitative variable?
A) Balance in your checking account
B) The life of a car battery
C) Eye color
D) Number of people employed by a company
Answer: C) Eye color

---

Q7. Discrete variables are typically the result of:
A) Measuring
B) Counting
C) Rounding
D) Estimating
Answer: B) Counting

---

Q8. Which of the following is an example of a continuous variable?
A) The number of bedrooms in a house
B) The number of students in a statistics course
C) Duration of flights from Orlando to San Diego
D) Number of strokes on a golf hole
Answer: C) Duration of flights from Orlando to San Diego

---

Q9. A frequency table groups qualitative data into classes that are:
A) Mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive
B) Random and overlapping
C) Numeric and continuous
D) Discrete and infinite
Answer: A) Mutually exclusive and collectively exhaustive

---

Q10. In the table with Location and Number of Cars (total 180), the relative frequency for Kane (52 cars) is:
A) 52
B) 0.289
C) 0.222
D) 0.250
Answer: B) 0.289 (52/180)

---

Q11. A graph that shows qualitative classes on the horizontal axis and class frequencies on the vertical axis, with bar heights proportional to frequencies, is called a:
A) Pie chart
B) Histogram
C) Bar chart
D) Frequency polygon
Answer: C) Bar chart

---

Q12. A chart that shows the proportion or percentage that each class represents of the total number of frequencies is a:
A) Bar chart
B) Pie chart
C) Histogram
D) Stem-and-leaf display
Answer: B) Pie chart

---

Q13. When constructing a frequency distribution, which of the following is NOT one of the listed steps?
A) Decide on the number of classes
B) Determine the class interval
C) Set the individual class limits
D) Calculate the median
Answer: D) Calculate the median

Q14. For the Wachesaw Manufacturing data (16 days of units), the range is calculated as:
A) 31 − 25 = 6
B) 28 − 25 = 3
C) 31 − 27 = 4
D) 30 − 26 = 4
Answer: A) 31 − 25 = 6

Q15. In the Wachesaw example, with 6 classes and a range of 6, the class width is:
A) 6

Q16. In the Wachesaw solution table, the class "25 − 26" has a frequency of:

Q17. In the Wachesaw solution table, the relative frequency for the class "27 − 28" is:

Q18. In the Wachesaw solution table, the cumulative frequency for the class "27 − 28" is:

Q20. In the profit frequency distribution table (total 180), which class has the highest frequency?
A) $1,400 up to $1,800 (38)
