Here are 20 multiple-choice questions based on Lecture 06 (Determinants and Inverse of a Matrix).

Q1. The determinant of A = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix} is:
A) ab - cd
B) ad - bc
C) ac - bd
D) ad + bc
Answer: B) ad - bc

Q2. The determinant of a matrix can be:
A) only positive
B) only negative
C) positive, zero, or negative
D) only zero
Answer: C) positive, zero, or negative

Q3. The minor M_{ij} of an element a_{ij} is:
A) the determinant of the matrix obtained by deleting the i-th row and j-th column
B) the element a_{ij} multiplied by (-1)^{i+j}
C) the sum of the i-th row and j-th column
D) the product of the diagonal entries
Answer: A) the determinant of the matrix obtained by deleting the i-th row and j-th column

Q4. The cofactor C_{ij} is given by:
A) M_{ij}
B) (-1)^{i+j}M_{ij}
C) (-1)^{i-j}M_{ij}
D) a_{ij}M_{ij}
Answer: B) (-1)^{i+j}M_{ij}

Q5. The adjoint of a matrix A is:
A) the cofactor matrix of A
B) the transpose of the cofactor matrix of A
C) the inverse of the cofactor matrix
D) the determinant times the identity matrix
Answer: B) the transpose of the cofactor matrix of A

Q6. For A = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \end{bmatrix}, the minor matrix M_A is:
A) \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 4 \\ -2 & 0 & 8 \\ -1 & -6 & 4 \end{bmatrix}
B) \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 4 \\ 2 & 0 & -8 \\ -1 & 6 & 4 \end{bmatrix}
C) \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -2 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & -6 \\ 4 & 8 & 4 \end{bmatrix}
D) \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 0 & 1 \\ 2 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \end{bmatrix}
Answer: A) \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 4 \\ -2 & 0 & 8 \\ -1 & -6 & 4 \end{bmatrix}

Q7. For the same matrix A, the cofactor matrix C_A is:
A) \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 4 \\ -2 & 0 & 8 \\ -1 & -6 & 4 \end{bmatrix}
B) \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 4 \\ 2 & 0 & -8 \\ -1 & 6 & 4 \end{bmatrix}
C) \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 4 \\ 2 & 0 & -8 \\ -1 & -6 & 4 \end{bmatrix}
D) \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 4 \\ -2 & 0 & 8 \\ 1 & -6 & 4 \end{bmatrix}
Answer: B) \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 4 \\ 2 & 0 & -8 \\ -1 & 6 & 4 \end{bmatrix}

Q8. If an entire row of a square matrix consists of zeros, then its determinant is:
A) 1
B) 0
C) undefined
D) equal to the product of the diagonal
Answer: B) 0

Q9. If two rows of a square matrix are equal, then its determinant is:
A) 1
B) 0
C) negative
D) equal to the diagonal product
Answer: B) 0

Q10. If one row of a square matrix is a multiple of another row, then its determinant is:
A) 1
B) 0
C) the multiple itself
D) undefined
Answer: B) 0

Q11. The determinant of a triangular matrix is:
A) the sum of the diagonal entries
B) the product of the diagonal entries
C) always zero
D) the product of all entries
Answer: B) the product of the diagonal entries

Q12. For an upper triangular matrix with diagonal entries 1, 2, and -4, the determinant is:
A) 1 + 2 - 4 = -1
B) 1 × 2 × (-4) = -8
C) 0
D) 8
Answer: B) 1 × 2 × (-4) = -8

Q13. Which statement about determinants is TRUE?
A) \det(A) = -\det(A^T)
B) \det(A) = \det(A^T)
C) \det(A^T) = 0 always
D) \det(A^T) = 1/\det(A)
Answer: B) \det(A) = \det(A^T)

Q14. If A and B are square matrices of order n, then \det(AB) =:
A) \det(A) + \det(B)
B) \det(A)\det(B)
C) \det(A) - \det(B)
D) \det(A)/\det(B)
Answer: B) \det(A)\det(B)

Q15. If A is an n \times n matrix and k is a scalar, then \det(kA) =
A) k\det(A)
B) k^n\det(A)
C) k^2\det(A)
D) \det(A)
Answer: B) k^n\det(A)

Q16. A square matrix A is invertible if and only if:
A) \det(A) = 0
B) \det(A) \neq 0
C) \det(A) = 1
D) \det(A) > 0
Answer: B) \det(A) \neq 0

Q17. If A is invertible, then \det(A^{-1}) =:
A) \det(A)
B) -\det(A)
C) 1/\det(A)
D) \det(A)^2
Answer: C) 1/\det(A)

Q18. If A and B are 3\times 3 matrices with \det(A) = 5 and \det(B) = 4, then \det(2A) =:
A) 10
B) 20
C) 40
D) 80
Answer: C) 40 (since 2^3 \times 5 = 40)

Q19. With \det(A) = 5 and \det(B) = 4 (both 3\times 3), \det(A^{-1}B^T) =:
A) 4/5
B) 5/4
C) 20
D) 1/20
Answer: A) 4/5 (since \det(A^{-1}) = 1/5 and \det(B^T) = 4)

Q20. The inverse of an invertible matrix A using the adjoint method is:
A) A^{-1} = \det(A) \cdot \text{adj}(A)
B) A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det(A)} \text{adj}(A)
C) A^{-1} = \text{adj}(A)
D) A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det(A)} A^T
Answer: B) A^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det(A)} \text{adj}(A)
