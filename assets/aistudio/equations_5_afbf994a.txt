Q1. Find L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{1}{s^2 + 4}\right\}.

A) \dfrac{1}{2}\sin 2t
B) \sin 2t
C) \dfrac{1}{2}\cos 2t
D) 2\sin 2t

Answer: A) \dfrac{1}{2}\sin 2t

---

Q2. To find L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{1}{s^2 + 4s + 3}\right\}, the denominator is rewritten as:

A) (s+2)^2 - 1
B) (s+2)^2 + 1
C) (s-2)^2 - 1
D) (s+2)^2 + 3

Answer: A) (s+2)^2 - 1

---

Q3. Find L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{1}{s^2 + 4s + 3}\right\}.

A) e^{-2t}\sinh t
B) e^{2t}\sinh t
C) e^{-2t}\sin t
D) e^{-2t}\cosh t

Answer: A) e^{-2t}\sinh t

---

Q4. State Theorem 4 relating the inverse Laplace transform of a derivative.

A) L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{dF(s)}{ds}\right\} = tf(t)
B) L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{dF(s)}{ds}\right\} = -tf(t)
C) L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{dF(s)}{ds}\right\} = -\dfrac{f(t)}{t}
D) L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{dF(s)}{ds}\right\} = \dfrac{f(t)}{t}

Answer: B) L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{dF(s)}{ds}\right\} = -tf(t)

Q5. Find L^{-1}\{\ln(s+3)\}.

A) -\dfrac{e^{-3t}}{t}
B) \dfrac{e^{-3t}}{t}
C) -\dfrac{e^{3t}}{t}
D) -te^{-3t}

Answer: A) -\dfrac{e^{-3t}}{t}

Q6. Find L^{-1}\left\{\ln\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{s^2}\right)\right\}.

A) \dfrac{2 - 2\cos t}{t}
B) \dfrac{2\cos t - 2}{t}
C) \dfrac{2 - 2\sin t}{t}
D) \dfrac{2 + 2\cos t}{t}

Answer: A) \dfrac{2 - 2\cos t}{t}

Q7. Find L^{-1}\{\tan^{-1}s\}.

A) -\dfrac{\sin t}{t}
B) \dfrac{\sin t}{t}
C) -\dfrac{\cos t}{t}
D) -\dfrac{e^{-t}}{t}

Answer: A) -\dfrac{\sin t}{t}

Q8. State the Second Shifting Property.

A) L^{-1}\{e^{-as}F(s)\} = u(t-a)f(t+a)
B) L^{-1}\{e^{-as}F(s)\} = u(t-a)f(t-a)
C) L^{-1}\{e^{as}F(s)\} = u(t+a)f(t+a)
D) L^{-1}\{e^{-as}F(s)\} = e^{at}f(t-a)

Answer: B) L^{-1}\{e^{-as}F(s)\} = u(t-a)f(t-a)

Q9. Find L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{e^{-5s}}{(s+3)^3}\right\}.

A) u(t-5)\dfrac{(t-5)^2 e^{-3(t-5)}}{2}
B) u(t-5)\dfrac{(t-5)^2 e^{3(t-5)}}{2}
C) u(t-5)\dfrac{(t+5)^2 e^{-3(t+5)}}{2}
D) u(t-5)\dfrac{(t-5)^3 e^{-3(t-5)}}{6}

Answer: A) u(t-5)\dfrac{(t-5)^2 e^{-3(t-5)}}{2}

---

Q10. Find L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{e^{-4s}}{s^2}\right\}.

A) (t-4)u(t-4)
B) (t+4)u(t+4)
C) t\,u(t-4)
D) (t-4)u(t)

Answer: A) (t-4)u(t-4)

Q11. Find L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{e^{-3s}}{s^2 + 9}\right\}.

A) \dfrac{1}{3}u(t-3)\sin 3(t-3)
B) \dfrac{1}{3}u(t-3)\sin 3t
C) u(t-3)\sin 3(t-3)
D) \dfrac{1}{3}u(t+3)\sin 3(t+3)

Answer: A) \dfrac{1}{3}u(t-3)\sin 3(t-3)

Q12. For a rational function \dfrac{P(s)}{Q(s)} with distinct linear factors Q(s) = (s-s_1)(s-s_2)\cdots(s-s_n), the partial fraction expansion has the form:

A) \dfrac{A_1}{s-s_1} + \dfrac{A_2}{s-s_2} + \dots + \dfrac{A_n}{s-s_n}
B) \dfrac{A_1}{(s-s_1)^2} + \dots + \dfrac{A_n}{(s-s_n)^2}
C) A_1(s-s_1) + A_2(s-s_2) + \dots
D) \dfrac{A_1 s}{s-s_1} + \dots + \dfrac{A_n s}{s-s_n}

Answer: A) \dfrac{A_1}{s-s_1} + \dfrac{A_2}{s-s_2} + \dots + \dfrac{A_n}{s-s_n}

Q13. For F(s) = \dfrac{s+3}{s(s-1)(s+2)}, the partial fraction constants are:

Q14. Find L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{s+3}{s(s-1)(s+2)}\right\}.

A) -\dfrac{3}{2} + \dfrac{4}{3}e^{t} + \dfrac{1}{6}e^{-2t}

Q15. For F(s) = \dfrac{2s-1}{s^3 - s}, the partial fraction constants are:

Q16. If Q(s) has a repeated linear factor (s-s_k)^n, the corresponding portion of the partial fraction expansion is:

Q17. For F(s) = \dfrac{s^2 + 9s + 2}{(s-1)^2(s+3)}, the correct form of the partial fraction expansion is:

Q18. In L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{e^{-5s}}{(s+3)^3}\right\}, which property is applied after finding L^{-1}\left\{\dfrac{1}{(s+3)^3}\right\}?

B) Second Shifting Property
C) Linearity
D) Theorem 4 (derivative of transform)

Answer: B) Second Shifting Property

Q19. In the partial fraction method, after multiplying by the denominator, the constants are often found by:

A) Setting s equal to the roots of the denominator and comparing coefficients
B) Differentiating both sides
C) Integrating both sides
D) Taking the Laplace transform again

Answer: A) Setting s equal to the roots of the denominator and comparing coefficients

Q20. To find L^{-1}\{\ln(s+3)\}, which theorem is used?

A) L^{-1}\{F'(s)\} = -tf(t)
B) L^{-1}\{e^{-as}F(s)\} = u(t-a)f(t-a)
C) L^{-1}\{F(s-a)\} = e^{at}f(t)
