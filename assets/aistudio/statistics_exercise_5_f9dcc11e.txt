20 MCQs from Lecture 5: Confidence Intervals Part 2

---

Q1. For large samples (n ≥ 30), the sampling distribution of \bar{X} is approximately normal due to:
A) The t-distribution
B) The Central Limit Theorem
C) The pooled variance
D) The chi-square distribution
Answer: B) The Central Limit Theorem

---

Q2. For small samples (n < 30), which distribution is used for confidence intervals?
A) Standard normal Z-distribution
B) Student's t-distribution
C) Chi-square distribution
D) F-distribution
Answer: B) Student's t-distribution

---

Q3. For a small sample, the degrees of freedom for the t-distribution is:
A) n
B) n + 1
C) n − 1
D) n − 2
Answer: C) n − 1

---

Q4. The t-distribution is:
A) Symmetric and bell-shaped with lighter tails than normal
B) Symmetric and bell-shaped with heavier tails than normal
C) Asymmetric with heavier tails than normal
D) Identical to the normal distribution for all sample sizes
Answer: B) Symmetric and bell-shaped with heavier tails than normal

---

Q5. As the sample size (and degrees of freedom) increases, the t-distribution:
A) Moves further from the normal distribution
B) Approaches the standard normal distribution
C) Becomes skewed
D) Becomes bimodal
Answer: B) Approaches the standard normal distribution

---

Q6. The t-random variable is defined as:
A) T = Z \cdot \sqrt{V/\nu}
B) T = Z / \sqrt{V/\nu}
C) T = \sqrt{V/\nu} / Z
D) T = Z + \sqrt{V/\nu}
Answer: B) T = Z / \sqrt{V/\nu}

Q11. For the difference between two population means (large samples), the standard error is:
A) \sqrt{s_1^2/n_1 - s_2^2/n_2}
B) \sqrt{s_1^2/n_1 + s_2^2/n_2}
C) (s_1 + s_2)/\sqrt{n}

Q16. The pooled standard deviation s_p is used when:
A) Sample sizes are large and variances are unequal
B) Small samples are used and population variances are assumed equal
C) Populations are not normal
