Twenty MCQs from Lecture 6: Applications of Laplace Transform

---

Q1. State the Laplace transform of the derivative y'(t).

A) L\{y'(t)\} = sY(s) + y(0)
B) L\{y'(t)\} = sY(s) - y(0)
C) L\{y'(t)\} = s^2Y(s) - y(0)
D) L\{y'(t)\} = sY(s) - y'(0)

Answer: B) L\{y'(t)\} = sY(s) - y(0)

---

Q2. State the Laplace transform of the second derivative y''(t).

A) L\{y''(t)\} = s^2Y(s) - sy(0) - y'(0)
B) L\{y''(t)\} = s^2Y(s) + sy(0) + y'(0)
C) L\{y''(t)\} = s^2Y(s) - y'(0)
D) L\{y''(t)\} = s^2Y(s) - sy'(0) - y(0)

Answer: A) L\{y''(t)\} = s^2Y(s) - sy(0) - y'(0)

---

Q3. Solve the IVP y'(t) + 2y(t) = 4e^{2t}, \quad y(0) = 1.

A) y(t) = e^{2t}
B) y(t) = e^{-2t}
C) y(t) = 2e^{2t}
D) y(t) = e^{2t} + e^{-2t}

Answer: A) y(t) = e^{2t}

---

Q4. In solving y'(t) + 2y(t) = 4e^{2t}, after taking Laplace transforms, Y(s) simplifies to:

A) \dfrac{1}{s-2}
B) \dfrac{1}{s+2}
C) \dfrac{s+2}{s-2}
D) \dfrac{4}{s-2}

Answer: A) \dfrac{1}{s-2}

---

Q5. Solve y' - 2y = e^{-t}, \quad y(0) = 0. What is Y(s)?

A) \dfrac{1}{(s+1)(s-2)}
B) \dfrac{1}{(s-1)(s+2)}
C) \dfrac{1}{(s+1)(s+2)}
D) \dfrac{1}{(s-1)(s-2)}

Answer: A) \dfrac{1}{(s+1)(s-2)}

Q6. For Y(s) = \dfrac{1}{(s+1)(s-2)}, the partial fraction expansion gives y(t) equal to:

A) -\dfrac{1}{3}e^{-t} + \dfrac{1}{3}e^{2t}

C) -\dfrac{1}{3}e^{t} + \dfrac{1}{3}e^{-2t}

Answer: A) -\dfrac{1}{3}e^{-t} + \dfrac{1}{3}e^{2t}

Q7. Solve the IVP y''(t) - 9y(t) = 0, \quad y(0) = -3, \quad y'(0) = 5. What is Y(s)?

Q10. Solve y'' - 4y' + 3y = e^{t}, \quad y(0) = 0, \quad y'(0) = 1. After taking Laplace transforms, the equation becomes:

A) (s^2 - 4s + 3)Y(s) = \dfrac{1}{s-1} + 1

Q11. For the IVP y'' - 4y' + 3y = e^t, y(0)=0, y'(0)=1, the denominator of Y(s) factors as:

Q12. The solution to y'' - 4y' + 3y = e^t, \ y(0)=0, \ y'(0)=1 is:

Q13. What is an integral equation?

A) An equation involving derivatives only
B) An equation in which an unknown function occurs in a definite integral
C) An equation with only algebraic terms
D) An equation involving infinite series

Answer: B) An equation in which an unknown function occurs in a definite integral

---

Q14. What is an integro-differential equation?

A) An equation where the unknown occurs only in integrals
B) An equation where the unknown occurs only in derivatives
C) An equation where the unknown occurs both in an integral and in differentiated form
D) An equation with no unknown function

Answer: C) An equation where the unknown occurs both in an integral and in differentiated form

Q17. In solving the integral equation x(t) = 2 + \int_0^t x(u)\,du, which property of the Laplace transform is used for the integral term?

A) L\left\{\int_0^t f(u)\,du\right\} = \dfrac{F(s)}{s}
B) L\left\{\int_0^t f(u)\,du\right\} = sF(s)
C) L\left\{\int_0^t f(u)\,du\right\} = F(s) - f(0)
D) L\left\{\int_0^t f(u)\,du\right\} = \dfrac{F(s)}{s^2}

Answer: A) L\left\{\int_0^t f(u)\,du\right\} = \dfrac{F(s)}{s}
